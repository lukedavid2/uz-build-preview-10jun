Why world rhymes the way it does

World is built around the back /ɔːr/ (/ɔːr/); it's one-syllable and closes on a hard stop. In our 54,000-word dictionary the perfect-rhyme pool returns 5 matches, family rhymes 0, additive and subtractive together 101, assonance 4,234, and consonance 305. That's a narrow strict column with a very deep slant well beneath it. Modern songwriting reads those slants as rhymes; the ear has been trained on them for a century. In the room with a guitar: write toward the strict rhyme first, then go back through and replace the obvious ones with the assonance matches that earned their place. World is a word that benefits from the second pass.
